Your ticket

  • Tickets are free. There are 15 seats.
  • One ticket covers both the Pretty and the Killer show, in the same seat.
  • Your seat is in My tickets on the device you got it on.
  • Can't make one of the shows? Tap Can't make it? in My tickets. You can keep your seat or give it back for someone else.

Seats

  • Center: 3 seats facing the stage
  • Left Side and Right Side: 4 seats each
  • Left Box and Right Box: 1 seat each
  • Left Box 2 and Right Box 2: 1 seat each

Merch stand

  • Ordered online? Pick it up at the merch stand. Give your name and your order number (like PK-4821).
  • Online orders are pre-orders, so you get them at the show.
  • Only at the stand: the Good Morning Sun Tee (Pretty show) and the Killer Glow Tee (Killer show).

During the show

  • See the songs and vote for the ones you want to hear on the tracklist.
  • The guestbook opens 50 minutes after each show starts. Leave Finn a message.
  • Take a picture with a Pretty or Killer frame in the photo booth.

Bring

  • Your order number if you bought merch
